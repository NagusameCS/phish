How to Protect Yourself

  • Check the URL — always look at the address bar before entering credentials.
  • Hover before you click — inspect link destinations in emails.
  • Use a password manager — it won’t auto-fill on the wrong domain.
  • Enable 2FA / MFA — even leaked passwords are useless without the second factor.
  • Report suspicious emails — your IT/security team wants to know.
